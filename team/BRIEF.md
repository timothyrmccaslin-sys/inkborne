# Inkborne demo: creative brief (Kekai, Creative Director)

## The project
Title: **Inkborne** (set by Timothy, final).

**SPEC is live: /workspace/inkborne/SPEC.md. It is the contract for keys, sizes, formats, palette, data schemas, and mechanic names, and it overrides anything below.** Mechanic names are already set in SPEC section 2 (Brushstroke, Clean Line, Vermilion Ink, Inkwing, Seal Arts, Still Water, Six Inks, mon). Writers do not rename them. The gauge is **Vermilion Ink** (locked; SPEC's old name is retired for IP reasons).
Playable browser demo (Rust/WASM, installable PWA) of an ORIGINAL JRPG that reimagines classic late-90s JRPG mechanics:
turn-based party battles, timed-press combo attacks, and a gauge that lets a character transform into a winged elemental form.

## Hard rules (IP)
- Story, world, cast, names, designs, and music are entirely our own.
- Do NOT use any Legend of Dragoon names, characters, story beats, designs, or music. Never use the word "Dragoon".
- Audio is synthesized or self-generated only. No outside samples.

## Visual style
- Sumi-e ink wash, same look as Timothy's Asura game, with red as the single accent color (seals, blood, lanterns, UI highlights).
- Style references on the shared box: /workspace/asura-ref/reference.jpg, daeva-reference.jpg, sorcerer-reference.jpg, and /workspace/asura-art/.
- Palette: paper off-white, ink black and grey washes, one vermilion red. Avoid other saturated colors except inside the transformation forms (elemental tint is allowed there, kept muted).
- Detail rises by view:
  1. WORLD MAP: large, bold, abstract brush strokes, like a painted folding screen.
  2. POINTS OF INTEREST (forest path, small town): walkable, subtle ambient motion (grass, mist, falling leaves, lanterns).
  3. ACTION SCREENS (battle, dialogue with portraits, shop): most detail and animation.

## Demo content
- Battle: 3-member party vs 1-3 enemies, and one transformation shown.
- Dialogue scene with portraits.
- Shop.
