# Inkborne: cast and flavor text (story team)
Source of truth: `CANON.md`. Ids match `team/systems` data.

## Party

### Itsuki Hane (`itsuki`): Wandering Swordsman
- **Ink:** Gale. **Inkwing:** the Gale Crane, with long white-and-grey crane wings, feathered ink edges, and a muted Gale tint.
- **Role:** physical damage. **Strokes:** Falling Pine, then Wet Edge. **Seal Art:** Gale Crane Script.
- **Personality:** Calm, guarded, and dry-witted. He woke in Sumikage Wood with no memory and a blank seal on his palm, and he quietly fears he's an unfinished drawing.
- **Look:** Tall and lean, in a loose grey kimono with a torn sleeve. Long black hair is tied low with a red cord, and his left palm is wrapped. He carries a brush-handled katana that leaves ink trails.
- **Bio:** Nobody in Hanshi knows his face, and the Blot doesn't seem to know what to do with him either. It slides off him the way rain slides off oiled paper.

### Benika Matsuo (`benika`): Shrine Archer
- **Ink:** Ember. **Inkwing:** the Ember Kite, with wide kite-bird wings smoldering at the tips, a muted Ember tint, and drifting ash.
- **Role:** healer and support at range. **Strokes:** Three Prayer Slips, then Lantern Volley. **Seal Art:** Ember Kite Litany.
- **Personality:** Bright and stubborn. She's fiercely protective, quick to scold, and quicker to laugh.
- **Look:** Short and sturdy, in a white shrine top and vermilion hakama, the cast's main red accent. Her hair is chin length with a paper charm clipped in, and her tall bow has prayer slips tied to it.
- **Bio:** She's the keeper of Hanshi's shrine at the top of the stair. When its seal cracked she took it as a personal insult, and she left with her bow before anyone could tell her not to.

### Gonzō Kuraishi (`gonzo`): Retired Temple Monk
- **Ink:** Stone. **Inkwing:** the Stone Owl, with heavy rounded owl wings like layered slate, a muted Stone tint, and cracks that glow faintly red.
- **Role:** tank and heavy hitter. **Strokes:** Temple Bell, then Mountain Gong. **Seal Art:** Stone Owl Verse.
- **Personality:** Gruff, slow to speak, and endlessly kind underneath. He eats too much and hums off-key.
- **Look:** Huge and broad, bald, with a thick grey beard and prayer beads the size of plums. He wears a patched dark robe, carries an iron-banded staff, and has a faded red seal tattooed across his back.
- **Bio:** Years ago he failed to hold a seal, and he has never forgiven himself. He says he's only coming along to carry the rice.

## NPCs
### Old Hotaru (`lantern_keeper`): Hanshi's lamplighter
A stooped, soft-spoken old man who relights the red lanterns every dusk and talks to each flame like a grandchild. He's the first to notice that the flames are greying.

### Grandmother Tsuru (`tsuru`): keeper of Kōbō Tsuru
A tiny, sharp-eyed shopkeeper who has sold tea and charms in Hanshi for longer than the lanterns have been red. She haggles for fun and always gives Gonzō an extra rice ball.

## Enemies
- **Blotling** (`blotling`): A small puddle of living ink with one blinking eye. It splits when struck hard. *"It doesn't hate you. It just wants you to be blank like it."*
- **Hollow Lantern** (`hollow_lantern`): A torn paper lantern drifting on ink smoke, its red flame going grey. Its dimming curse lowers accuracy. *"Once it lit the way home. Now it forgets where home was."*
- **Smudge Ronin** (`smudge_ronin`, demo mini-boss): A samurai whose outline was half-unwritten by the Blot. He fights with a dripping blade and has no face, only a wet brushstroke. *"He still guards the seal-stone. He no longer remembers why."*

## Places
- **Sumikage Wood** (`sumikage` node, `forest` POI): An ink-shadow wood of bamboo and old pines, with drifting mist and falling leaves, and broken seal-stones along the path.
- **Hanshi** (`hanshi` node, `town` POI): A small border town of paper-walled houses and red lanterns, with Benika's shrine at the top of the stair.

## Mechanics note
- The transformation gauge is **Vermilion Ink**, which is locked.
- **Hakushi Clearing** (`woods_edge`): The paper-white clearing where Itsuki woke. It's the start of the demo.
- **Shuin Stair** (`shrine_stair`): Ninety-nine seal-stamped steps up to Benika's shrine. It's locked in the demo.
- **Usuzumi Shore** (`grey_shore`): A beach where the sea is fading to thin grey ink. It's locked in the demo.
